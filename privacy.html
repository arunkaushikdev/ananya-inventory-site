Who we are

Ananya Inventory is billing, stock and GST software for Windows, made by Arun Datt Kaushik ("we", "us"). This policy covers the Ananya Inventory desktop app, this website, and the licence service that activates the app.

Questions or requests about your data: arun.idevs@gmail.com.

The short version

  • Your business data (items, parties, bills, ledgers, reports) is stored on your own computer. We do not receive it, and we cannot read it.
  • The app talks to our licence service only to check your trial or licence. It sends a device identifier, not your business data.
  • If you fill a form on this website, we receive what you type, and we use it to contact you about Ananya Inventory.
  • We do not sell personal data and we do not run advertising or tracking cookies.

Data in the desktop app

Everything you enter in Ananya Inventory is saved in a database on your computer: company details, items and stock, parties and ledgers, sale and purchase bills, GSTIN, PAN, licence numbers, staff users, and reports. This stays on that computer.

  • Backups. Backups go to the folder you choose. We do not upload them anywhere.
  • Several computers in one shop. If you use the Main PC and other PCs option, data moves only between computers on your own network.
  • Staff PINs. PINs are kept on your computer. We cannot recover a forgotten PIN for you.
  • Printing, import and export. Files you create or import stay where you save them.

Because we do not hold your business data, you are responsible for keeping your computer and backups safe. See the Terms of Use.

Licence and trial checks

To run a free trial or a paid licence, the app contacts our licence service. This is the only information the app sends to us:

  • your activation code or licence key, when you activate or renew;
  • a device identifier. It is a one-way hash made from your computer, so it cannot be turned back into your hardware details;
  • your computer name, as shown in the app, so you can recognise a device when you manage your licence;
  • the app version, and the date your trial started.

We use this to limit a licence to the number of computers you bought, to apply trial days, and to tell you when a licence expires. We do not receive bills, parties, items, or any figures from your business.

The app can also be activated offline with a code, without contacting our service.

Buying a licence

When you buy or renew online, you pay on the payment page of our payment partner, Razorpay. Razorpay collects and processes your card, UPI or net banking details under its own privacy policy. We never see or store your full card number, UPI PIN or bank password.

We receive your name, email address, phone number, the plan and amount, and the payment reference. We keep these to issue your licence key, send you a receipt, handle renewals and support, and meet tax and accounting rules. We may email you the licence key.

This website

  • Forms. When you book a demo or contact us, we receive the details you enter: name, shop or company, mobile number, email, city, business type, the software you use today, and your message. The form is delivered to us as email through FormSubmit, a form-handling service. We use the details only to reply to you, arrange a demo, and follow up about Ananya Inventory.
  • Fonts. Pages load a font from Google Fonts. Google receives your IP address and browser details when it serves the font.
  • Hosting. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may keep standard server logs such as IP addresses.
  • Cookies and analytics. The site does not set cookies and does not use analytics or advertising trackers.

Why we use data, and our legal basis

We use personal data to provide and secure the software and licence, reply to your enquiries, process payments and issue invoices, keep records the law requires, and improve the product based on what you tell us. We process it because you gave it to us for these purposes, to carry out a purchase you asked for, or because the law requires it, in line with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and other Indian law that applies.

Who we share data with

We share personal data only with the services that help us run Ananya Inventory: Razorpay for payments, our email service for sending receipts and licence emails, the form service named above, and our hosting providers. They may process data only to provide their service. We may also disclose data if a court or authority lawfully requires it. We do not sell personal data.

How long we keep it

Enquiry details: until your request is handled, and up to 24 months after if you may still become a customer. Licence and payment records: as long as the licence exists and for the period tax and accounting law requires. After that we delete or anonymise them. Data on your own computer stays until you delete it.

Security

The licence service uses encrypted connections. Licences are signed so they cannot be edited, and our signing keys are not part of the app. No system is perfectly secure, so we cannot promise absolute security, but we work to protect the data we hold.

Your rights and choices

You can ask us to tell you what personal data we hold about you, correct it, or delete it, and you can withdraw consent for things like follow-up emails at any time. Write to arun.idevs@gmail.com from the email address you used. We aim to reply within 30 days. If you are not satisfied with our answer, you may complain to the Data Protection Board of India once it is operational.

To remove a computer from your licence, use Settings → Licence in the app, or write to us.

Children

Ananya Inventory is business software and is not meant for children. We do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone under 18.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top. If a change is significant, we will also tell customers by email or inside the app.
