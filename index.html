Made for chemists who cannot afford an expired strip

Every batch carries its expiry. The bill picks the earliest-expiring stock first, and warns you before you sell something risky.

  • First-expiry-first-out batch selection on every sale
  • Expired and near-expiry batches are marked in red and amber
  • Schedule H, H1 and narcotic items flagged, with doctor and patient details on the bill
  • Party-wise control over which scheduled drugs a customer may be billed
