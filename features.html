Every feature, in plain words

What Ananya Inventory does today, grouped by the job you are doing.

Billing

The counter screen, built for speed.

  • Sale bills with item search, batch picker and live stock
  • Point of sale screen for walk-in customers
  • Quotations, sale orders and delivery challans
  • Credit notes and debit notes against the original bill
  • Save, hold and copy bill lines from the keyboard
  • Bill levies and round-off on the final amount

GST and tax

Right tax on every line, ready for your CA.

  • CGST and SGST for local sales, IGST for central sales
  • Sales types with a tax slab per bill, plus item-wise HSN tax
  • A tax error warning when an item's tax differs from the bill
  • GSTIN, place of supply and state-wise tax handling
  • TDS and TCS on bills
  • GST reports for return filing

Pharmacy tools

For stores where expiry and schedules matter.

  • Batch-wise stock with expiry and MRP
  • First-expiry-first-out batch selection
  • Expired and near-expiry batches highlighted
  • Schedule H, H1 and narcotic flags on items
  • Doctor, patient and prescription number on the bill
  • Party-wise rules on which scheduled drugs can be sold

Items and stock

Know exactly what is on the shelf.

  • Item master with HSN, packing, unit, rack, company and salt
  • Rate A, B and C, purchase rate, MRP and minimum margin
  • Minimum, maximum and re-order levels
  • Godowns, stock transfers and stock adjustment
  • Barcode labels
  • Item-wise discounts and free-scheme offers

Parties and ledgers

Customers and suppliers, with their full story.

  • Create ledger with address, GST, licence and credit details
  • Bill-wise opening balances
  • Credit limit and credit days
  • Party status panel with balance, last sale and last receipt
  • Company discount and special rates per party
  • Attach documents to a party

Accounts

Daily books without a separate accounting package.

  • Receipts and payments against bills
  • Cash book and bank book
  • Expenses, contra, journal and cheque register
  • Bank reconciliation
  • Party statements and outstanding
  • Profit and loss

Reports

Open a report and get your answer.

  • Sales and purchase books
  • Stock status and expiry reports
  • Outstanding and ageing by party
  • Daily analysis and today's gross profit
  • Re-order list
  • Print or export the numbers you need

Control and safety

Your shop, your rules.

  • Staff users with PIN sign-in
  • Roles: owner, accountant, salesman, store keeper, viewer
  • Hide purchase rates and profit from billing staff
  • Backup and restore
  • Import from Tally
  • Auto capital letters while typing, with a switch to turn it off
